# Brand Experience Agentic Capability {#brand-experience-agentic-capability}

The Brand Experience Agentic Capability accelerates the modernization and evolution of digital experiences by automating content updates, site transformation, and technical troubleshooting, enabling brands to continuously improve and adapt at the speed of AI.

## Agentic Capabilities {#agentic-capabilities}

The Brand Experience Agentic Capability delivers its outcomes through the following agentic capabilities, each specializing in a specific set of skills:

* [Experience Production Agentic Capability](#experience-production) - Reduces time-to-market with content updates, content creation, Figma to Visual Content Fragment imports, form creation, and communications
* [Development Agentic Capability](#development) - Provides AI-assisted troubleshooting and build automation
* [Onboarding Agentic Capability](#onboarding) - helps you configure Adobe Experience Manager Assets using natural language

Through these agentic capabilities, the Brand Experience Agentic Capability absorbs the heavy operational work behind updates and modernization, helping teams deliver changes faster and with greater confidence in today's AI era.

## Experience Production Agentic Capability {#experience-production}

[The Experience Production Agentic Capability](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/experience-production/use-cases.md) automates high effort and high-volume tasks. It Empowers teams and turns manual, weeks-long processes into fast, AI-assisted workflows that keep every experience current and consistent, helping the business achieve its goals.

### Content Update Skill {#content-update}

[The Content Update skill](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/experience-production/content-update.md) updates existing content — including content fragments, pages, forms and assets — with ease. The Agentic Capability can perform actions such as updating, removing, replacing, or adding content elements to keep experiences accurate and current. Inputs can be natural language description, and when used with Jira PDFs and screenshots can provide input too.

### Content Create Skill {#content-create}

The [Content Create](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/experience-production/content-create.md) skill creates new pages based on a marketing brief, a template and a natural language prompt.

### Figma to Visual Content Fragments Skill {#figma-to-visual-content-fragments}

The [Figma to Visual Content Fragments](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/experience-production/figma-to-visual-content-fragments.md) skill imports Figma designs to AEM Content Models, Content Fragments and HTML templates.

### Forms Creation Skill {#forms-creation}

[The Forms Creation skill](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/experience-production/forms/forms-use-cases.md) removes the friction from building adaptive forms and personalized correspondence by transforming natural language requirements into fully structured, branded outputs, empowering business users to create scalable, data-driven experiences without dependency on development or IT teams.

## Experience Development Agentic Capability {#experience-development}

[The Experience Development Agentic Capability](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/development/use-cases.md) streamlines technical evolution within AEM by assisting with template updates, component configuration, and troubleshooting, reducing dependency on lengthy development cycles.

### Pipeline Troubleshooting Skill {#pipeline-troubleshooting}

The pipeline troubleshooting skill removes the friction from technical changes and issue resolution by identifying root causes, recommending fixes, and accelerating implementation — helping teams modernize and optimize experiences without slowing innovation.

## Onboarding Agentic Capability {#onboarding}

[The Onboarding Agentic Capability](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/onboarding/use-cases.md) helps you configure Adobe Experience Manager Assets using natural language, coordinating the full onboarding lifecycle, from environment provisioning through content structure, metadata, migration, and search.
