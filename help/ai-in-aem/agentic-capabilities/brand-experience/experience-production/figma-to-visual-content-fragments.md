# Figma to Visual Content Fragments Skill {#figma-to-visual-content-fragments-skill}

The Figma to Visual Content Fragments skill of the [Experience Production Agentic Capability](/help/ai-in-aem/agentic-capabilities/brand-experience/experience-production/overview.md) automates the process of recreating Figma designs in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) as a Cloud Service.
