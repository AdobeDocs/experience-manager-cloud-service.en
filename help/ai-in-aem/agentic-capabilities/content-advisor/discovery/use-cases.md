# Discovery Agentic Capabilities {#discovery-agentic-capabilites}

The Discovery Agentic Capability of Adobe Enterprise Manager (AEM) as a Cloud Service collaborates with [Coworker Chat](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/cx-enterprise-ai/experience-cloud-ai/coworker/chat/overview) to deliver AEM content on demand through natural, conversational prompts for a streamlined, click-free discovery experience. It intelligently searches across Assets, Content Fragments, AEM Sites pages, and Adaptive Forms to deliver relevant materials such as images, videos, PDF documents, articles, and form templates.
