# Content Advisor Agent {#content-advisor}

The Content Advisor Agent removes the friction from evolving content by absorbing the heavy operational work behind content discovery and optimization, helping teams deliver changes faster and with greater confidence for today's AI era.

## Agents {#agents}

The Content Advisor Agent delivers its outcomes through the following agents, each specializing in a specific set of jobs:

* [Content Discovery](#discovery)
* [Content Optimization](#optimization)

The Content Advisor Agent empowers teams to turn manual, long processes into fast, AI-assisted workflows that keep content current and consistent, helping the business achieve its goals.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>AI-generated responses may be inaccurate or misleading. Be sure you double-check suggested fixes and responses. 
>
>See also [Adobe Experience Cloud Generative AI User Guidelines](https://www.adobe.com/legal/licenses-terms/adobe-dx-gen-ai-user-guidelines.html)

### Content Discovery Agent {#discovery}

[The Content Discovery Agent](/help/ai-in-aem/agents/content-advisor/discovery.md) interprets user intent, searches across AEM for the most relevant, approved content, and surfaces it for reuse, connecting the right assets to the right moment. 

### Content Optimization Agent {#optimization}

[The Content Optimization Agent](/help/ai-in-aem/agents/content-advisor/content-optimization.md) personalizes and adapts visuals, images, and videos for different channels, improving engagement and conversion across the experience lifecycle. 

## Use with Other Agents {#other-agents}

The agent is able to work with other agent skills.
