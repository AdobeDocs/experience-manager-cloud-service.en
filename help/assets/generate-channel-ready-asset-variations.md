# Generate channel-ready asset variations using Content Optimization Agent {#generate-channel-ready-asset-variations}

Content Optimization Agent (COA) in Content Hub enables you to generate channel-ready asset variations using natural language prompts. The agent automatically applies channel-specific optimizations, such as dimensions, composition, and enhancements, to prepare assets for distribution across multiple channels.

After generating the variations, you can download individual assets, download all generated variations, or copy their URLs for sharing.

## Prerequisites {#prerequisites}

Before using Content Optimization Agent, ensure that:

* You have access to Content Hub.
* Content Optimization Agent (Beta) is enabled for your organization.

## Generate channel-ready asset variations {#generate-variations}

Generate optimized asset variations directly from Content Hub by performing the following steps:

1. Sign in to **Content Hub**.
2. Search for and select the asset that you want to optimize.
3. Click **[!UICONTROL Content Optimization Agent]**.

   ![Launch Content Optimization Agent from Content Hub](assets/content-hub-launch-coa.png)

4. Review the suggested prompts.
5. Select one of the suggested prompts or enter your own prompt using natural language.
6. Click **[!UICONTROL Generate]**.

   Content Optimization Agent automatically creates channel-ready variations by applying:

   * Channel-specific dimensions
   * Intelligent composition
   * Image enhancements
   * Optimizations for the selected channel

   ![Generate channel-ready asset variations](assets/content-hub-coa-generate-variations.png)

7. Wait for the generation process to complete.

   ![Generated channel-ready asset variations](assets/content-hub-generated-variations.png)

## Download or share generated variations {#download-share-generated-variations}

After the generation process completes, you can:

* Download an individual variation.
* Copy the URL of a generated variation to share it with other users.
* Download all generated variations in a single action.

![Download or share generated asset variations](assets/content-hub-download-share-variations.png)

## Benefits {#benefits}

Content Optimization Agent helps you:

* Generate channel-ready asset variations without leaving Content Hub.
* Eliminate repetitive resizing and formatting tasks.
* Prepare optimized assets for multiple channels in seconds.
* Improve content consistency across digital channels.
* Accelerate campaign delivery.

## Limitations {#limitations}

* This capability is available as a Beta feature.
