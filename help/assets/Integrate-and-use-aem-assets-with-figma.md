Integrate [!DNL AEM Assets] with [!DNL Figma] to access and use [!DNL AEM] assets in your [!DNL Figma] design workflow through an asset selector panel.

The free Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Assets Connector allows you to access your organization's assets directly within Figma. Easily browse, search, filter, and drag and drop assets from AEM Assets into your Figma designs – all without leaving your workflow.

You can also export your final designs from Figma and upload them directly to designated folders in AEM Assets, making it simple to share, manage, and version your creative work across teams.

Supported formats (will be expanded in future releases):

Import from AEM Assets to Figma: JPG, PNG.
Export from Figma to AEM Assets: PNG, PDF, JPG, SVG.

## Before you begin{#prerequisites-for-aem-assets-and-figma-integration}

Fulfil the following requirements to integrate [!DNL AEM Assets] with [!DNL Figma]:

1. Access to the following Assets Cloud Service environments: 
    1. Assets Essentials UI
    1. Assets enterprise Cloud Service environment
    1. Assets Prime or Ultimate licence

## Import [!DNL AEM Assets] plugin into [!DNL Figma]{#import-aem-assets-plugin-into-figma}

Execute the following steps to import the [!DNL AEM Assets] plugin in [!DNL Figma]:

1. Navigate to [[!DNL Figma] community](https://www.figma.com/community) page, click Plugin and select Import and export to display Import & export plugins page.
1. Search Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Assets Connector in the search bar and select Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Assets Connector.
1. Click **[!UICONTROL Open in...]** to select a file to open or click **[!UICONTROL New file]** to create a new file.
1. Click Run and sign-in to your experience manager to open the **[!UICONTROL AEM Assets connector]** panel to drag the assets on the panel and drop them on the canvas.

## Import [!DNL AEM] assets into [!DNL Figma] workflow{#import-aem-assets-into-figma-workflow}

Execute the following steps to import the [!DNL AEM] assets into your [!DNL Figma] workflow:

1. Open your desktop app
1. Click Actions from the menu bar at the bottom of the canvas and select **[!UICONTROL Adobe Experience Manager AEM Assets connector]** to select [!DNL AEM] aasets from the panel.
1. Drag and drop the asset on the canvas.
